# Business Context — F016_WordFamily

## Why It Matters

Learning vocabulary in isolated word lists is inefficient — students forget words faster when they're not connected to related concepts. Word Family groups related words around a shared root (e.g. "act" → "action", "active", "reaction") so students build a richer mental map of English vocabulary in one pass. AI generation means the catalog of root-word families can grow on demand instead of requiring manual content creation, and the bridge into flashcards lets students immediately apply spaced-repetition study to what they just learned.

## Who Uses It

- **Students** — browse categories, generate fresh word families for a topic, save favorites, and turn selected words into a flashcard deck for ongoing practice.
- **Admins** — curate the category catalog (create, rename, reorder, retire categories) that structures how students discover word families.

## What They Do

1. A student opens Word Family and browses categories (e.g. "Shopping & Restaurant", "Travel & Entertainment").
2. Inside a category, the student sees existing root words as chips, or taps "Generate more" to have AI create new ones — the system automatically avoids generating words that already exist in that category.
3. The student taps a root word to see its full family: related words, part of speech, meaning in English and Vietnamese, an example sentence, and how the word is built from prefix/root/suffix.
4. The student can save the word family to their personal collection for later, and select which words they want to turn into flashcards.
5. Tapping "Create Flashcards" builds a new flashcard deck from the selected words, ready for spaced-repetition study alongside the student's other decks.
6. Separately, an admin manages the list of categories — adding new topics, editing names/descriptions, or retiring outdated ones — through a dedicated admin screen.
