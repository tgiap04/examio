# Business Context — F004_ProfileManagement

## Why It Matters

Profile management lets users shape their identity on the platform by setting their name, username, bio, avatar, and banner image. A clear profile builds trust in academic and collaborative environments. Session management enables users to see all their active login sessions across devices and terminate any that look suspicious or belong to devices they no longer use.

## Who Uses It

- **Student** — updates their display name, avatar, and bio to build their identity on the platform
- **Student** — changes their account password when they want to improve security
- **Student** — reviews the list of devices and browsers where their account is currently logged in, and logs out of a specific device they no longer use

## What They Do

1. Student navigates to their profile page and views their current name, username, avatar, banner, and bio.
2. Student edits their name, username, or bio text fields and clicks Save — the changes are reflected immediately across the platform.
3. Student clicks on their avatar or banner image to upload a new photo — the image is stored and the new URL is saved to their profile.
4. Student navigates to the password change section, enters their current password and a new password, and clicks Save — an email confirmation is sent and the password is updated.
5. Student views the list of active sessions, sees the browser, operating system, and approximate location of each device, and clicks Delete on an old device to terminate that session remotely.
