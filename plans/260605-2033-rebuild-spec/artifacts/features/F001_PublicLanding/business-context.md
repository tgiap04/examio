# Business Context — F001_PublicLanding

## Why It Matters

The landing experience is the platform's first impression for every visitor. It showcases the value of the platform — quiz sets, flashcard decks, and exam rooms — so that guests can evaluate whether to register. Static informational pages build trust by making the platform's legal standing and contact information transparent.

## Who Uses It

- **Visitor (guest)** — browses public content, reads about the platform, and decides whether to register or log in
- **Returning user (authenticated)** — lands on the home page to quickly access their dashboard or browse public content from another device

## What They Do

1. Visitor arrives at the home page and sees a featured content grid with quiz sets, flashcard sets, and exam rooms published by other users.
2. Visitor clicks the Login or Register button in the navigation CTA, which takes them to the authentication pages.
3. Visitor browses informational pages (About, Terms, Privacy, Cookies, Security) to learn more about the platform before deciding to register.
4. Visitor fills out the contact form with their name, email, and message to reach the platform team with questions or feedback.
